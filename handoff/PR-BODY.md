## What this changes

Documentation and brand assets only. No code, no config, no contract changes.

### README

- Reframes the opening around what Dotify is for: common spaces full of people
  listening alone, and music as a cultural commons that bridges them. The
  artist sovereignty pillar is stated up front alongside the social one.
- Adds a **Screenshots** section with the catalog, the listening rooms galaxy
  and a live room.
- Adds a **Roadmap** section in priority order: full portability to Product
  DevNet, subscription as commitment (staking based), ambassador program,
  decentralised music awards.
- Drops the hardcoded track count from "What works" - the catalog grows, so the
  number goes stale.
- Adds `brand/`, `docs/images/` and `docs/presentation/` to the Structure table.

### New assets

- `brand/` - the Dotify mark (Polkadot ring of six dots with a stylised G clef
  at the centre) in light and dark variants, horizontal and stacked lockups,
  banners with the tagline, one-colour versions, app icons and favicons. The
  clef is vector outlines, so no music font is needed anywhere. Usage notes are
  in `brand/README.md`.
- `docs/images/` - the three product screenshots used by the README.
- `docs/presentation/` - the project presentation deck as PDF.
