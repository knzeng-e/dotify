## Limits and next gate

Physical Desktop 0.1.3 / Mobile 0.10.0 Product-host visits and a real artist's
API/RPC-verified announcement remain deployment acceptance. No fresh signature
is requested per visit: missing/expired sessions explain reconnect or discreet
entry. Badges establish the registered publishing account at entry, not a
real-world identity or ongoing ownership, and grant no access or host rights.

The dashboard counts connected sessions in rooms whose host declares playback.
It does not claim historical plays, unique humans or verified audibility. Room
reconnect privacy remains unchanged: host-disconnected rooms disappear until
resumed. Social state is ephemeral and dies with the room/server. Confirmed tips
reuse the existing supported receipt verification path; unsupported native
receipt notifications are not synthesized from client success messages.

Full PR and post-merge `dev` quality gates passed before publication, including
the complete Playwright room flows. The public Product wrapper loaded the
published application frame without browser errors. These checks establish the
deployed web and signaling surfaces; they do not replace the physical-host
acceptance above.
