## Work sequence

1. Review consented pilot observations before adding mechanics. Compare community curation, repeat-host support, artist-controlled export/alternative key operator, and consensual memory of a room.
2. For provenance or ambassador recognition, define what the event proves, who consents, retention/deletion, spam/Sybil resistance, and how artists can opt out. Do not equate referral volume with cultural value.
3. For artist recovery/portability, specify export format, policy and royalty continuity, authenticated key handover, alternative hosting, and a restore rehearsal. Distinguish recoverability from censorship resistance.
4. Write a decision record and one small implementation ticket for the highest-evidence need. Preserve awards/NFT rewards as optional future choices; never automatically enroll listeners or turn discovery into a financial leaderboard.
