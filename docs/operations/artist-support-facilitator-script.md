# Artist Publication and Support Facilitation Script

Use this script for an authorized pilot session only. Do not fill answers from
inference, screenshots, wallet addresses, or automated test output. Record real
answers only after the participant consents to the session record.

## Artist Questions

Ask after the artist reaches the **New release** review step, before signing.

1. Which wallet controls this release?
2. What access condition will listeners meet?
3. If the release is paid, who receives support and what share does each
   recipient receive?
4. What does Dotify still need before the release is considered published?
5. If you reject the signature or the catalog is slow to refresh, what can you
   safely retry?

Expected understanding: the participant can identify the controller, access
mode, support amount and asset, split recipients, network-fee prompt, and the
registry plus catalog read-back boundary. They should not describe upload alone
or a transaction hash alone as a published release.

## Listener Questions

Ask after the listener sees the Classic access gate and again after any support
attempt finishes.

1. What amount and asset would be sent before you open the track?
2. Who benefits from the support?
3. What changes between pending, canceled, failed, included-but-unverified, and
   confirmed states?
4. What access did your wallet acquire after a confirmed receipt?
5. Does this receipt promise perpetual media availability or immediate receipt
   by every collaborator?

Expected understanding: the participant can identify the runtime-native amount,
recipient split, transaction link when present, and that Dotify opens protected
audio only after runtime read-back confirms playable access. They should know a
recipient share may be claimable instead of immediately received.
